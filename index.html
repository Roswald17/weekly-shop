How to read this

● verified prices were checked online in October 2026. ○ est. prices are estimates, so replace them with the shelf price on your first shop. After one week of real receipts, the totals will be accurate.

Quantities assume 2 eggs, a glass of protein milk, one tin of sardines and one chicken portion a day. Staples like butter, olive oil and rice last longer than a week, so only that week's share is counted.

Aldi doesn't sell groceries online in Ireland, so most Aldi prices can only be checked in store.
